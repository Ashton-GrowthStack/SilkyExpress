About Silky Express

Silky Express was founded on a simple belief: businesses shouldn't have to sacrifice quality or pay premium prices to source products globally. We started after witnessing firsthand how complex, costly, and time-consuming traditional sourcing from China could be.

Our journey began when our founders realized that the gap between factory prices in China and what companies pay internationally was unnecessarily large. Through deep relationships built over years in China's manufacturing hubs, we discovered an opportunity to bridge that gap — and Silky Express was born.

Today, we leverage our network of verified manufacturers, our quality control expertise, and our logistics capabilities to deliver a simple promise: access to quality products from China at 30-50% lower costs than traditional channels. We handle the complexity so you can focus on growing your business.

Whether you're a startup needing 100 units or an established company looking to reduce costs on a product line, Silky Express is here to make global sourcing simple, transparent, and profitable.
